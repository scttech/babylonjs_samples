# Asteroids 3D models

The 3D models used by the Asteroids samples (starting with [Asteroids Next Steps](../asteroids_next_steps/README.md)). The rocket and the two UFOs are CC0 models from Pixabay; the fighter was made with Meshy.

| File | Used for |
|---|---|
| `arunangshubanerjee-rocket-ship-2090.glb` | The player's ship (the Rocket) |
| `spaceship.glb` | The player's other ship (the Fighter) |
| `plaggy_cc0-ufo-621.glb` | The large UFO |
| `jeremywoodsster-ufo-2582.glb` | The small UFO |

## Changes made to the files

The meshes are untouched. To keep the files small, the textures inside the rocket, the fighter and the large UFO were resized (the rocket's textures from 2048 × 2048 pixels, the large UFO's from 4096 × 4096): color maps to 1024 × 1024 JPEG, and normal and metallic-roughness maps to 512 × 512. That took the rocket and the two UFOs from 17.7 MB to about 1.6 MB, and the fighter, which was about 9 MB, to 3.2 MB. The small UFO has no textures and is unchanged.
